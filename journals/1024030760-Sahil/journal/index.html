    Weekly Progress Journal - Sahil Soni¶

    • Name: Sahil Soni
    • Roll No: 1024030760
    • Project Name: Solar Sage AI
    • Role: UI/UX & Frontend Developer

    Week 1 (Aug 3 - Aug 9): UI/UX Ideation & System Flow Planning¶

    • Collaborated on understanding the solar-panel maintenance problem and defining the MVP requirements for Solar Sage AI.
    • Studied the overall workflow involving drone inspection, computer vision, AI-agent decisions, and automated cleaning.
    • Designed the initial UI/UX structure for presenting panel conditions, inspection results, and system decisions.
    • Planned the application navigation, dashboard structure, and reusable frontend components.

    Week 2 (Aug 10 - Aug 16): Wireframing & Frontend Environment¶

    • Set up the frontend development environment and organized the project structure.
    • Created wireframes for the main monitoring dashboard and panel-analysis interfaces.
    • Designed layouts for displaying solar-panel images, detected defects/dirt levels, and inspection results.
    • Planned the visualization of AI-agent outputs and important system metrics in a user-friendly manner.

    Week 3 (Aug 17 - Aug 23): Dashboard & Panel Analysis Interface¶

    • Developed the initial frontend dashboard for monitoring solar-panel inspection activities.
    • Implemented UI components for displaying individual panel analysis, including panel scores and inspection information.
    • Worked on interfaces for displaying computer-vision results and panel conditions.
    • Designed the frontend flow for moving from image inspection to AI-based recommendations and cleaning actions.

    Week 4 (Aug 24 - Aug 30): AI Decision & Data Visualization Integration¶

    • Integrated dynamic data into the frontend to display AI-agent decisions and panel-level analysis.
    • Developed UI components for representing panel priority, dirt levels, efficiency gains, and other relevant metrics.
    • Worked on visualizing the relationship between detected panel conditions and recommended cleaning actions.
    • Improved the dashboard's navigation, responsiveness, and consistency across different screens.

    Week 5 (Aug 31 - Sep 6): Dashboard Refinement & Final Testing¶

    • Refined the overall dashboard design to provide a professional and cohesive monitoring interface.
    • Implemented and polished views for batch analysis, panel-level results, and performance comparisons.
    • Added visual representations of important metrics such as dirt score, efficiency gain, and savings.
    • Conducted frontend testing to ensure accurate data rendering and smooth navigation.
    • Finalized UI/UX refinements and prepared the frontend for the final project demonstration.

    Note: The dashboard shown on page 6 of the PDF report reflects this work, including panel analysis results, performance comparison, batch analysis, dirt scores, efficiency gains, and savings.
