    ☀️ Solar Sage AI

    A drone-based Edge-AI system for condition-based solar panel cleaning — replacing blind, water-heavy maintenance with intelligent, targeted action.

    🤖 Edge AI 🛩️ Drone-Based 👁️ OpenCV ⚡ ESP32 📊 Dashboard
    <1s Decision Latency
    70%+ Water Savings
    5 AI Agents
    99% Uptime Target
    ## 🔍 The Problem

    Solar operators at utility scale face a critical mismatch — panels are cleaned on fixed schedules, blind to actual dirt levels. This leads to:

    • 💧 Massive water waste — a 1 GW farm uses 12.5M+ litres/month on washing alone
    • 📅 Schedule blindness — clean panels get washed; genuinely dirty ones wait weeks
    • ⚠️ Physical degradation — frequent contact cleaning causes micro-cracks and shortens panel life
    • 📉 Fragmented decisions — without per-panel data, the dirtiest panels aren't prioritized
    ## 💡 Our Solution

    Solar Sage AI is an autonomous "Inspect-to-Clean" system that uses drone imagery and edge AI to clean only what needs cleaning, when it needs cleaning.

    📷

    Perception Pipeline

    RGB & thermal drone payload with OpenCV-based detection to capture and segment panel imagery in real time.

    🧠

    Edge Decision Layer

    ESP32-driven control system classifying dirt levels and issuing cleaning commands with sub-second latency.

    🤖

    Multi-Agent Backend

    Confidence, priority, benchmarking, ROI, and water-optimization agents that jointly decide when and where to clean.

    🎯

    Targeted Actuation

    Servo-controlled, precision water application — replacing blanket cleaning cycles with surgical interventions.

    📊

    Live Dashboard

    Per-panel dirt scores, efficiency gains, cost savings, and ROI reporting — all in a single monitoring interface.

    🔄

    CI/CD Pipeline

    Automated builds and tests for the OpenCV pipeline and ESP32 firmware on every change, enabling safe iteration.

    ## ⚙️ Processing Pipeline
    1 🛩️

    Drone Capture

    RGB & thermal imagery across the panel field

    2 👁️

    OpenCV Detection

    Segment panels, detect surface dirt & debris

    3 🧠

    AI Decision

    Score dirt 0–100, rank by cleaning priority

    4 ⚡

    ESP32 Execution

    Servo & pump signals for targeted cleaning

    5 ✅

    Validate

    Confirm cleaning, log metrics for ROI
